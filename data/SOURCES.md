**Not in this pack:** Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories (DoD), foreign per diem (State Department), actual-expense authorizations above 300% of per diem, NYC occupancy **percent** / NY-NYC sales tax (not printed on the cited NYC.gov Hotel Tax page).

**Not a billing determination.** Contract travel clauses and your company's policy win if they conflict with these ceilings. Federal travelers may be lodging-tax exempt in some cities — confirm before bidding tax as zero.
